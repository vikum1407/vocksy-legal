Delete Your Vocksy Account

Vocksy lets you permanently delete your account and all associated data (vehicles, service history, expenses, fuel logs, photos) at any time.

Option 1 — In the app

Open Vocksy, go to Profile / Settings → Delete My Account, and confirm. This is the fastest method.

Option 2 — No longer have the app installed

Use our account deletion request page with your registered mobile number:

Request Account Deletion

What happens next

  • Your account is scheduled for deletion in 14 days.
  • If this request wasn't made by you, simply log back in to the Vocksy app within those 14 days to cancel it — we'll also notify your account immediately when a request like this is made.
  • After 14 days, your account, vehicles, and all associated history are permanently deleted. Any booking history shared with a garage or vehicle owner you've interacted with is kept on their side, with your identifying details removed.
